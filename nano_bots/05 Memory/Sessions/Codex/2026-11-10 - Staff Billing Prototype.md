# Staff Billing Prototype (2026-11-10)

Reviewed the Front Desk and Accounting Billing configuration, invoice summary,
folio collection action, and deposit verification rules. The interactive
preview prioritizes balances due at checkout, groups pending deposits, and
shows the selected folio with charges, applied payments, and its next action.

The sample Front Desk role can collect an eligible in-house balance.
Accounting can review and apply the sample pending deposit. Checkout totals
and the visible folio update when a sample action completes.

Edge checks passed at 320, 390, 800, and 1,056 px in light and dark. They
covered filtering, folio search, payment amount validation, the sample
receipt, and the role-specific deposit workflow. Delivered for design review.
