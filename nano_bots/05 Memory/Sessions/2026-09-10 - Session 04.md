# 2026-09-10 — Session 04: Customer Payments & Folio filters + refinement

## Work completed

Refined `/account/payments` (customer portal): added URL-driven folio filters and an
elevated visual pass, keeping the coastal card identity (refinement, not redesign;
Impeccable Operate mode).

- **Filters** — two chip rows above the list: stay timeline (`?stay=`
  current/upcoming/past/cancelled) and payment state (`?pay=` pending/due/refund/settled).
  Chips are plain `<Link>`s (back-button/share/direct-load work, zero client JS) with live
  counts; zero-match chips render dimmed/disabled; invalid values mean "all".
- **Aggregate strip** — filtered folio count, outstanding balance, paid-to-date,
  server-computed.
- **Per-folio progress bar** — paid-vs-total bar (accent = partial, green = settled) with
  `progressbar` role + text alternative, between card header and `.folio-summary`.
- **Per-filter empty state** — names the active filters, one-click "Show all folios".

## Decisions

- Filter state lives in the URL, not client state — server-rendered, no hydration cost.
- Payment-state precedence: in-flight payment > live balance > refund bookkeeping >
  settled (an in-flight payment outranks the balance it covers).
- `folioMoney` centralizes the page's invoice-fallback math so card and aggregate can't drift.
- No SYSTEM.md change — it documents the route but not this page's presentation detail.
