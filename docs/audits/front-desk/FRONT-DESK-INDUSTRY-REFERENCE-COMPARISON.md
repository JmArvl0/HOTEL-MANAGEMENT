# Industry comparison and feature gaps

The vendor workflows below establish comparable operating practices. Property
policy governs payment gates, inspection, authorizations, rates and cutoff
times. Comparisons use official vendor documentation opened for this audit.

| Workflow | HAVEN behavior | Industry workflow | Gap or decision | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Arrival, in-house and departure queues | Separate queues in the Front Desk dashboard, Bookings and controlled readiness flow | OPERA groups arrivals, in-house, departures and room searches in Front Desk. [Oracle Front Desk](https://docs.oracle.com/en/industries/hospitality/opera-cloud/26.1/ocsuh/ch_front_desk.htm) | Keep the queues. Link headline metrics directly to the matching operational filter, and keep the counter and detail on the same population. | P2 |
| New reservation | Walk-in and other sources share a staged form, with a saved-guest search, live room-type quote and review | Cloudbeds exposes multiple entry points and lets an operator find availability as part of creating a reservation. [Cloudbeds reservation creation](https://myfrontdesk.cloudbeds.com/hc/en-us/articles/217997248-Create-a-new-reservation-in-Cloudbeds-PMS) | Keep the staged checks. Retain the selected queue and stay context when entering the form from Bookings, Calendar or a guest profile. Preserve distinct Walk-in and Phone source handling. | P2 |
| Check-in readiness | Server enforces status, contact, identity, balance and eligible-room checks, with occupancy and age gates configured by the property | OPERA offers configurable steps for reservation, profile, identity, room assignment and payment. Scanning requires a separate integration. [Oracle check-in](https://docs.oracle.com/en/industries/hospitality/opera-cloud/26.2/ocsuh/t_checking_in_reservations.htm) | Consolidate visible blockers into one operator checklist. Automatic room allocation and external ID scanning are optional and require property acceptance. | P2 |
| Reservation calendar | Rooms and reservation calendar exist. List and Calendar share booking scope controls. Shared reservation detail opens a modal. | Cloudbeds keeps the calendar visible with a reservation panel and can pin frequently used actions. [Cloudbeds calendar operations](https://myfrontdesk.cloudbeds.com/hc/en-us/articles/48384262863771-Manage-reservations-from-the-New-Calendar) | Prioritize early access to the grid and a compact contextual panel. Preserve the selected room, dates and filters when opening a reservation. | P1 / P2 |
| Room moves and assignment | Front Desk can assign and move within type. Cross-type changes use authorized approval, room suitability, rate and guest acceptance rules. A room move retains the folio. | Cloudbeds surfaces candidate rooms and rate impact, and can retain completed nights on the original room. [Cloudbeds moves and assignment](https://myfrontdesk.cloudbeds.com/hc/en-us/articles/55835270255131-Move-and-assign-reservations-on-the-New-Calendar) | Show occupancy, fees and impact beside the candidate room. Dragging is optional. Keyboard-confirmable controls and strong server-side conflict handling are essential. | P2 |
| Folio settlement and checkout | Front Desk records authorized collection against the reservation. Checkout requires a settled folio and creates turnover. Accounting owns adjustments and payout. | OPERA's departure workflow supports searching from a dashboard tile, reviewing charges and settling billing windows. [Oracle checkout](https://docs.oracle.com/en/industries/hospitality/opera-cloud/25.1/ocsuh/t_checking_out_reservations.htm) | Keep reservation-scoped financial actions and direct return to checkout after settlement. Show offending duplicate references and unresolved balances. Split billing windows are property-specific. | P2 |
| Housekeeping readiness | Checkout marks the room dirty and creates a department task. Cleaning and optional inspection precede availability. | Cloudbeds combines room condition, Front Desk status and assignment. Inspection and allowed actions depend on property settings and permissions. [Cloudbeds housekeeping](https://myfrontdesk.cloudbeds.com/hc/en-us/articles/25695101078427-Manage-Housekeeping-in-Cloudbeds-PMS) | Keep the role boundary. Surface the current inspection blocker and link reception to the room's task without allowing unauthorized completion. | P2 |
| Close day and handover | Shared cash, report submission and handovers are separate workflows from authorized hotel business-day close. | OPERA uses sequenced business-date close procedures, with configurable open cashier and no-show behavior. [Oracle end of day](https://docs.oracle.com/en/industries/hospitality/opera-cloud/25.5/ocsuh/c_endofday_procedures.htm) | Keep the hotel's approved operating-day authority. Show what blocks the handoff and who owns it. An automatic no-show or cashier close is a policy decision. | P2 |

## Feature disposition

| Classification | Features and operational case |
| --- | --- |
| Already implemented | Reservation detail and folio, quick walk-in entry, guest history and saved-guest search, room status labels, arrival checklist, booking calendar, Manager approvals, shift handover and room turnover |
| Essential | Accurate idempotent extensions, visible current blockers, server-controlled room and money gates, protected role boundaries |
| Highly recommended | Compact workstation list and calendar, matched KPI drill-down, toast placement that leaves actions clear, persistent queue context within the session |
| Useful enhancement | Shared reservation side panel, direct links between room/folio/guest with return context, a lightweight priority rail, expected-versus-ready room view |
| Optional | Configured quick actions and keyboard shortcuts, manual context-aware allocation suggestions, authorized structured handover summaries |
| Redundant if added | A second reservation calendar, a separate generic guest-history system, reception-wide Accounting ledger, duplicate shift report surface, duplicate housekeeping conditions |
| Outside the workstation audit | Dedicated mobile Front Desk redesign, public or guest UI, external card capture and Fleet dispatch, payments and privacy decisions awaiting property approval |
