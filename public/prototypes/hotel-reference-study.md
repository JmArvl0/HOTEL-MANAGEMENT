# HAVEN hotel landing reference study

Reviewed November 3, 2026. This is a representative comparison, not an exhaustive
audit of every hotel website. Seven official sites were accessible through page
extraction; selected pages were also opened in a browser. Ace Hotel's direct page
returned 403 to the research tool, so its indexed content is recorded as limited
context and not a verified visual reference. No conversion or performance tests
were conducted on these sites. Cookie notices and delayed media limited some
browser views.

| Official reference | Observed content / navigation | Application to HAVEN |
|---|---|---|
| [The Hoxton](https://thehoxton.com/) | Prominent Find a Room action, date/guest booking controls, destination browsing and offers. Browser review showed warm framing and a large photographic/video hero. | Make the booking path easy to find; separate atmosphere from room selection. Do not copy promotions, multi-location controls or guarantees. |
| [Aman](https://www.aman.com/) | Reserve action, destinations, seasonal experiences and curated journeys. Browser review showed a centered identity and restrained navigation; media was partially obstructed by consent UI. | A small, confident header and a clearly separate reservation action. Keep only HAVEN's single-property navigation. |
| [Ett Hem](https://www.etthem.se/) | Direct room/table booking and a concise personal presentation. Its browser opening used distinctive illustration and restrained copy rather than a standard hotel hero. | Give the page a point of view; avoid unnecessary promises and generic luxury claims. Its artwork is not copied. |
| [Rosewood](https://www.rosewoodhotels.com/en/default) | Reserve, dining/wellness navigation and story-led discovery content. Browser content was only partially rendered during review. | Treat experiences as editorial content with clear links, not repeated icon cards. |
| [Four Seasons](https://www.fourseasons.com/) | Check Rates action, featured properties and experience categories. Page-content review only. | Clear hierarchy between searching rates, exploring accommodation and learning about the stay. |
| [The Henry](https://www.thehenryhotel.com/) | Philippine boutique-hotel positioning, reserve actions and destination content. Page-content review only. | Keep hospitality personality and direct-booking visibility; do not introduce unsupported HAVEN locations or heritage claims. |
| [Dusit](https://www.dusit.com/) | Date/guest rate search, booking management, wellness, dining and experience sections. Page-content review only. | Keep the guest's practical tasks visible alongside the atmospheric story. |
| [Ace Hotel](https://acehotel.com/) | Indexed content describes hotel locations, events and cultural programming; direct fetch was blocked. | Limited context only. No visual conclusion or layout was taken from the blocked page. |

## Design synthesis — interpretation, not measured superiority

The applicable pattern is atmosphere first, booking clearly available, room
comparison next, then amenities/experiences and practical arrival information.
HAVEN is one property, so chain selectors, global offers and corporate navigation
would add unnecessary friction. The proposal uses an original two-part hero:
large poolside image and short title on one side, a room photograph and room link
on the other. A simple date/guest form follows immediately.

The interactive room finder is now a dedicated section. Selecting any of the
four rooms updates the photograph, room name, capacity, snapshot rate and room
search link together. This avoids competing with the first impression. The
experience switcher, eight amenities, guest-account access, existing reservation
FAQs and contact information remain.

## Asset and business limits

No competitor artwork, photography, logos, copy, review, promotion or policy was
copied into HAVEN. Existing illustrative images are reused and labeled. Snapshot
rates and service caveats remain. Real location/contact details, unique property
photography and any genuine guest-review content still need verification before
production implementation. The current live landing is unchanged.
